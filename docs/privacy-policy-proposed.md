# Threshold Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026.

Understand what you share, who can see it, and how to get a copy of your records or leave Threshold.

## Optional email and text alerts

When notification delivery is available, you can choose training alerts on the Notifications page. We store your choices, delivery records, and, if you opt into texts, your phone number and verification, consent, and opt-out timestamps. Resend processes email alerts; Twilio processes phone verification and text alerts. Alerts link to Threshold without including private workout details. Turn individual alerts off on Notifications, remove your phone there, or reply STOP to stop texts. Your coach and athletes control their own choices. Text alerts are optional and are not required to use Threshold.

## Who is responsible for this service

Threshold LLC, a Utah limited liability company, operates Threshold and is responsible for the account and training information described in this notice. Contact us at [tad.s@mythreshold.co](mailto:tad.s@mythreshold.co) with privacy questions or requests.

## What Threshold stores

Threshold stores the profile and sign-in information needed for your account, the training and race details you enter, check-ins and notes, coach connections and messages, and records imported from integrations you choose to connect. Training records can include wellbeing scores and activity location, heart rate, pace, distance, and elevation when a connected provider supplies them.

## How your information is used

We use these records to run your account, show your training history, support the coach and athlete workflow, reconcile billing, troubleshoot failures, and respond to support requests. Session cookies keep you signed in. Browser storage caches account details so navigation responds quickly. Diagnostic logs may record technical information about requests and errors.

## What a connected coach can see

An active coach connection lets that coach review the training records used in the coaching workflow, including your profile and race context, check-ins, logged interventions, planned and completed workouts, and information you exchange in messages. Coaches can add plans, feedback, and coach notes. Only connect to a coach you intend to share this information with.

Review or disconnect a coach from Account Settings → Coach Connection. Disconnecting prevents ongoing connected access; it cannot remove copies or messages the coach already received. Contact support if you need help with previously shared records.

## Service providers and connected accounts

Threshold uses Supabase for account and database services, Vercel for hosting, and Stripe for payments. When configured, Resend delivers account emails and Sentry receives error reports, performance information, and session replays used to investigate problems. These services process information needed for their function. Card payment details are handled by Stripe. If you connect Strava or another supported provider, its own privacy terms also apply; connecting gives Threshold permission to retrieve the data covered by that authorization. Strava may collect information about API usage for its own purposes, including service improvements, support, and compliance with its agreement.

## Garmin Connect

When Garmin connectivity is available and you choose to connect your account, the information exchanged depends on the features you use and the permissions you authorize:

- **Activity API:** completed activity records from Garmin Connect, used to review your training.

- **Health API:** health and wellness records from Garmin Connect, used to view that information alongside your training.

- **Women’s Health API:** menstrual-cycle and pregnancy tracking information from Garmin Connect, used to view that information alongside your training. This notice does not by itself authorize sharing it with a coach.

- **Courses API:** selected courses and route information sent from Threshold to Garmin Connect for use with compatible devices.

- **Training API:** selected workouts and training plans sent from Threshold to Garmin Connect for use with compatible devices.

Health and reproductive information can be sensitive. Activity and course locations can reveal where you exercise or spend time. This policy is not consent to collect, share, or send that information. Your express authorization is required before transferring your information to Garmin.

Transfers take place through Garmin’s APIs between Threshold and Garmin Connect. Sending a course or workout discloses that information to Garmin. Garmin’s handling of information in Garmin Connect is described in the [Garmin Connect privacy policy](https://www.garmin.com/privacy/connect). Do not upload information to Garmin if you are restricted from doing so or change terms attached to that information if you are not permitted to change them. Do not send us your Garmin password.

### Limits on Garmin data use

We do not use Garmin data to train, fine-tune, or operate third-party artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. We do not sell or license Garmin data, or share it with advertising networks, data brokers, or external monetization services.

### Disconnecting Garmin

When a Garmin connection is disconnected, we delete the stored Garmin tokens and historical Garmin data held by Threshold. This does not delete information held independently by Garmin.

## Artificial intelligence

AI-generated coaching assistance is deferred in the current pilot. The restrictions on Garmin data above apply to third-party AI and machine-learning training, fine-tuning, and operation. This notice does not request consent to use Garmin information for AI processing or model training.

## Your records and choices

Account Settings → Your data provides a JSON download of your profile and personal training records. The download excludes provider credentials, uploaded file contents, coach-only notes, and other athletes’ records; it lists unavailable sections if an older deployment lacks a feature. For additional access requests, correction, deletion help, or questions about shared records, email [tad.s@mythreshold.co](mailto:tad.s@mythreshold.co). We may need to verify account ownership before acting.

## Privacy requests

Depending on the law that applies to you, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, object to or restrict its use, or withdraw consent for processing based on consent. Contact [tad.s@mythreshold.co](mailto:tad.s@mythreshold.co) to make a request or ask which choices apply to your records. A request about information held independently by a connected provider may also need to be directed to that provider.

## Deletion and retention

The account deletion control removes the account and associated training records handled by Threshold’s deletion workflow and first attempts to stop its linked Stripe billing. A failure is shown so you can retry or request help. Provider-held records, messages already received by others, diagnostic records, and backups may have separate retention or deletion processes. We do not promise immediate removal of every copy. Contact support about anything the self-service workflow does not cover.

Email [tad.s@mythreshold.co](mailto:tad.s@mythreshold.co) with privacy questions. This notice describes the current pilot and will be updated when the service or its data practices change.
